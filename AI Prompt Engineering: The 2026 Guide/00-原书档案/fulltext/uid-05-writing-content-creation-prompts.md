# Chapter 5: Writing & Content Creation Prompts

“Writing is the most common AI use case — and also the most misunderstood. These 65 prompts will help you generate high-quality content that’s actually useful, not just ‘AI-sounding’.”

## The Writing Prompt Formula

Most writing prompts fail because they’re missing key elements.

### The 5-Part Writing Prompt Formula

\[Task\] + [Topic/Subject] + [Format/Length] + \[Audience\] + [Tone/Style]

Bad prompt (missing 4 elements): “Write a blog post about marketing.”

Good prompt (all 5 elements): “Write a 700-word blog post about AI marketing strategies for small business owners, using a professional but approachable tone with real-world examples.”

### Why This Works

| Element | Purpose |
| Task | What exactly to create |
| Topic | What to write about |
| Format/Length | Structure and size |
| Audience | Who will read it |
| Tone/Style | How it should sound |

### Common Writing Mistakes

1. No format specification. “Write about email marketing” → could be 5,000 words. “Write a 300-word email newsletter about email marketing” → precise.

2. No audience definition. “Write about productivity” → generic. “Write about productivity tools for remote software developers” → targeted.

3. No tone guidance. “Write a product description” → could be anything. “Write a product description for a premium SaaS tool targeting enterprise buyers, professional but enthusiastic tone” → on point.

## Blog & Article Writing (15)

### 51. The Standard Blog Post

"Write a \[word count\]-word blog post about \[topic\] for \[audience\].
Include \[number\] H2 headings and \[number\] bullet points.
Tone: \[tone\]."

### 52. The Listicle

"Write a list post about \[topic\] with \[number\] items.
Format: H2 for each item, 150 words explaining each.
Target audience: \[audience\]."

### 53. The How-To Guide

"Write a step-by-step guide for \[task\].
Include \[number\] steps with detailed explanations.
Target audience: \[audience\]. Tone: \[tone\]."

### 54. The Comparison Article

"Compare \[product A\] and \[product B\] for \[use case\].
Include pros/cons for each, pricing information,
and a recommendation. Word count: \[number\]."

### 55. The Case Study

"Write a case study about how \[company\] achieved \[result\]
using \[method\]. Include specific metrics, challenges,
and lessons learned. Format: \[number\] paragraphs."

### 56. The Thought Leadership Piece

"Write a thought leadership article about \[trend\] in \[industry\].
Include 3 key insights, predictions for the next 12 months,
and actionable advice. Tone: professional, authoritative."

### 57. The Problem-Agitation-Solution (PAS)

"Write a PAS-style blog post about \[problem\].
1) Describe the problem.
2) Agitate the pain points.
3) Introduce a solution with [product/service].
Word count: \[number\]."

### 58. The Storytelling Post

"Write a narrative blog post about \[topic\] using a personal story.
1) Hook with personal experience.
2) Lessons learned.
3) Broader implications.
Tone: conversational, engaging."

### 59. The FAQ Format

"Create an FAQ page about \[topic\] with \[number\] questions.
Each answer 100–150 words. Target audience: \[audience\].
Tone: helpful, informative."

### 60. The Newsjacking Post

"Write a timely blog post connecting \[current event\] to \[topic\].
Include 3 takeaways for readers. Tone: relevant, insightful.
Word count: \[number\]."

### 61. The Guest Post Pitch

"Write a guest post pitch email to \[publication\] about \[topic\].
Include: hook, outline, and why it's relevant to their audience.
Tone: professional, persuasive."

### 62. The SEO-Optimized Post

"Write a 1,000-word SEO-optimized blog post about \[topic\].
Include: keyword-rich title, H2s featuring \[target keyword\],
LSI keywords \[list\]. Tone: \[tone\]."

### 63. The Evergreen Post

"Write an evergreen blog post about \[timeless topic\].
Focus on fundamentals that won't become outdated.
Include practical examples. Tone: authoritative, helpful."

### 64. The Contrarian Post

"Write a contrarian blog post challenging conventional wisdom
about \[topic\]. Include counter-arguments and evidence.
Tone: bold, thought-provoking."

### 65. The Recap Post

"Write a recap post about [event/development].
1) What happened. 2) Why it matters. 3) What's next.
Tone: informative, concise. Word count: \[number\]."

## Social Media Content (15)

### 66. The LinkedIn Post

"Write a LinkedIn post about \[topic\].
Format: hook question, 2–3 short paragraphs,
ending with a thoughtful question. Tone: professional, insightful.
Hashtags: \[number\]."

### 67. The Twitter/X Thread

"Write a thread about \[topic\] with \[number\] posts.
Each post: 240 characters max.
1) Hook. 2) \[number\] content posts. 3) Conclusion with link/question."

### 68. The Instagram Caption

"Write an Instagram caption for \[photo topic\].
1) Engaging hook. 2) 2–3 sentences of value.
3) Call to action. 4) \[number\] relevant hashtags. Tone: \[tone\]."

### 69. The TikTok / Reels Script

"Write a 60-second short-form video script about \[topic\] for \[audience\].
Format: 3-second hook, 40 seconds of value, 10-second CTA.
Include 3 visual cues. Tone: \[tone\]."

### 70. The Newsletter Intro

"Write a newsletter introduction for \[topic\].
Hook: question or surprising fact.
Body: 2–3 short paragraphs.
Close: preview of next issue. Word count: \[number\]."

### 71. The Carousel Post

"Create a LinkedIn carousel outline about \[topic\] with \[number\] slides.
Each slide: title, key point, visual idea. Tone: \[tone\]."

### 72. The Quote Post

"Write an insightful quote about \[topic\],
followed by 2–3 sentences of context.
Tone: \[tone\]. Optimized for Instagram or LinkedIn."

### 73. The Poll Prompt

"Create a LinkedIn poll about \[topic\] with \[number\] options.
Include: poll question, answer options, and a follow-up
comment explaining why the results matter."

### 74. The Story Starter

"Write a LinkedIn story starter about \[topic\].
1) Tease the story. 2) Ask a question. 3) Invite comments.
Tone: \[tone\]."

### 75. The AMA Announcement

"Write an AMA announcement for \[expert\] about \[topic\].
Include: who, when, what to prepare, how to participate. Tone: \[tone\]."

### 76. The Case Study Snippet

"Write a LinkedIn post teasing a case study about \[result\].
1) The result. 2) The method in one sentence.
3) 'Full case study in comments' CTA."

### 77. The Tip Post

"Write a tip post about \[topic\].
1) Hook question. 2) \[number\] tips with short explanations.
3) CTA to share. Tone: helpful, concise."

### 78. The Behind-the-Scenes

"Write a behind-the-scenes post about [process/project].
1) What people think happens.
2) What actually happens.
3) Why the difference matters. Tone: authentic, transparent."

### 79. The Milestone Celebration

"Write a milestone celebration post for \[achievement\].
1) What happened. 2) Gratitude. 3) Looking forward. Tone: \[tone\]."

### 80. The Question-Driven Post

"Write a question-driven LinkedIn post about \[topic\].
1) Opening question. 2) 2–3 related questions.
3) Invitation for discussion. Tone: \[tone\]."

### 94. The Networking Email

## Editing & Refinement (5)

### 111. The Tone Adjuster

"Rewrite this text with a [more/less] [formal/casual/professional]
tone. Keep the same meaning and length."

### 112. The Jargon Remover

"Rewrite this text to remove jargon and make it accessible
to \[audience\]. Keep the same meaning and length."

### 113. The Conciseness Improver

"Shorten this text to \[percentage\]% of the original length
while keeping the key points and meaning."

### 114. The Flow Improver

"Improve the flow and readability of this text.
Use transition words, vary sentence length,
and ensure logical progression."

### 115. The Grammar & Style Pass

"Review this text for grammar, punctuation, and style.
Fix errors while preserving the original tone and meaning."

## Chapter Summary

Writing with AI is a skill. These 65 prompts give you the toolkit to create content that’s useful, not just generated.

Core principles:

1. Always specify format and length.
2. Define your audience clearly.
3. Guide the tone explicitly.
4. Iterate and refine.
