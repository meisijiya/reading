# About the Authors

The AI Prompt Engineering Team is a collective of practitioners, researchers, and working professionals who ship prompts every day — across engineering, marketing, research, design, and operations. The team maintains an evolving library of prompts that survive contact with production traffic, real customers, and real revenue goals.

This book captures the patterns that held up across thousands of hours of real-world use. It is a living document. As the models change, so will the patterns. The principles — clarity, context, structure, iteration — do not.

### A final note

Prompt engineering in 2026 is less a technical skill and more a literacy. Like writing itself, it rewards the habit of doing it every day, noticing what works, and sharing what you learn.

Thank you for reading. Now go build something.

— The AI Prompt Engineering Team, 2026
