# Appendix H: Your 30-Day Prompt Engineering Plan

A daily schedule to take you from beginner to confident practitioner.

## Week 1 — Foundations

Day 1. Read Chapter 1. Write down three tasks in your current work that an AI could help with. Pick one.

Day 2. Read Chapter 2. Create free accounts on two chatbots (ChatGPT and Claude). Ask each the same question; compare.

Day 3. Read Chapter 3 (4 C’s). Rewrite three prompts you’ve used this week applying all 4 C’s.

Day 4. Chapter 4. Use 5 chat prompts on a real project. Save the best to a notes file.

Day 5. Chapter 5. Draft one blog post (Prompt 51) and one LinkedIn post (Prompt 66) using the templates.

Day 6. Chapter 5. Write a cold email sequence (Prompts 81–83) for something you actually need to send.

Day 7. Review. Which prompt saved you the most time? Which surprised you?

## Week 2 — Analysis and Research

Day 8. Chapter 6. Pick a spreadsheet you already have. Run Prompts 116 and 117.

Day 9. Use Prompt 124 (SWOT) on your own business or a side project.

Day 10. Competitive intelligence day. Run Prompts 156–159 on a real competitor.

Day 11. Try a prompt chain: 117 → 125 → 136.

Day 12. Market research. Prompt 126 (market size) on your product.

Day 13. Customer persona. Prompt 128 in detail.

Day 14. Review. Did the analysis change any decision you’re about to make?

## Week 3 — Technical and Creative

Day 15. If you write code: Prompts 166–168. If not: image generation Prompts 211–215.

Day 16. Code explanation or image iteration. Ask the model to explain one file or iterate one image 5 times.

Day 17. Unit tests or style exploration. Prompt 177 for code; 251–260 for images.

Day 18. Security review (Prompt 184) or prompt for a polished final deliverable.

Day 19. Build your first prompt chain in Zapier, Make, or n8n.

Day 20. Run a prompt library audit — save your top 10 prompts with names.

Day 21. Review. Which prompts got copy-pasted the most?

## Week 4 — Advanced and Business

Day 22. Chapter 9. Try chain-of-thought on a hard problem you’ve been avoiding.

Day 23. Self-consistency prompting on a decision you’re facing.

Day 24. Persona prompting — use the dual-persona technique.

Day 25. Constraint-driven prompt. Force exactly 500 words, exactly 5 bullets.

Day 26. Meta-prompting. Use AI to rewrite your worst-performing prompt.

Day 27. Chapter 10. Pick 3 business prompts (271–320) and run them.

Day 28. Automation. Wire one prompt into Zapier or Make.

Day 29. Appendix G. Try XML-structured prompting or JSON-mode output.

Day 30. Reflection. What does your team now do in minutes that used to take hours? Write a note to your future self about which prompts to never forget.
