## Pricing Patterns (Consumer Subscriptions)

Most flagship consumer plans cluster around $20/month, with premium/pro tiers around $100–$200/month for heaviest users. API pricing is billed per million input and output tokens, with flagship models typically more expensive than mid-tier models by 3–5x. Prompt caching and batch APIs can dramatically reduce cost on repeated or non-urgent workloads.

Always verify current prices on the provider’s website before committing.
