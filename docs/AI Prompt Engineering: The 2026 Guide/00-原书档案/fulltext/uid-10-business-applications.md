# Chapter 10: Business Applications

“Prompt engineering isn’t just a technical skill — it’s a business multiplier. These 50 prompts will help you save time, increase revenue, and scale your operations.”

## Business Use Cases Overview

Prompt engineering delivers ROI in three main areas:

| Area | Time Saved/Week | Revenue Impact | Example |
| Content creation | 10–20 hours | Higher output | Posts, emails, social |
| Analysis & research | 5–10 hours | Better decisions | Market, competitive, customer |
| Operations | 15–30 hours | Cost reduction | Docs, reporting, automation |

### The Business Prompt Framework

\[Business Goal\] + \[AI Capability\] + \[Specific Application\] + \[Expected Outcome\]

Example:

Goal: Increase blog output
Capability: Content generation
Application: 4 blog posts / week
Outcome: +50% website traffic in 3 months
